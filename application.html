Application for membership.

You can download and print the application, then drop off your completed packet at Station 1, 311 N 5th Street, Wills Point, Texas 75169, or email it with your supporting documents to officers@wpvfd.net. You can also fill out the application below and submit it instantly when online submission is available.

Dear Applicant,

We welcome your application for membership with the Wills Point Volunteer Fire Department. This packet explains the application process and contains the forms that must be completed before your application can be considered.

Please complete every section fully, accurately, and honestly. Integrity is essential to the fire service, and the information provided in this packet may be verified as part of the membership review process.

The Fire Department operates under an established chain of command. Members are expected to conduct themselves professionally, protect the safety and welfare of the public, attend emergency responses when available, participate in drills and regular meetings, support public education and community events, and continue developing their fire, rescue, emergency medical, and hazardous materials skills.

If you have questions about the application process or about serving with the Wills Point Volunteer Fire Department, contact the Department at (903) 873-3011 or visit Station 1.

Thank you for your interest in serving our community.

Sincerely,
Ed Leipply, Fire Chief
