Fire Prevention & Safety

Preventing emergencies before they happen is an important part of our mission. We encourage every home and business to take simple steps to reduce the risk of fires and other emergencies.

Smoke Alarms

  • Install smoke alarms inside every bedroom, outside sleeping areas, and on every level of your home.
  • Test smoke alarms regularly.
  • Replace alarms according to the manufacturer's recommendations.
  • Develop and practice a home fire escape plan.

Carbon Monoxide Safety

  • Install carbon monoxide alarms in appropriate areas of your home.
  • Never operate generators, grills, or fuel-burning equipment inside a home, garage, or enclosed area.
  • If a carbon monoxide alarm activates, leave the building and call 911.

Home Fire Safety

  • Never leave cooking unattended.
  • Keep combustible materials away from heaters and other heat sources.
  • Keep matches and lighters away from children.
  • Never overload electrical outlets or extension cords.
  • Know at least two ways out of your home whenever possible.

Outdoor Burning & Wildfire Safety

Dry vegetation, high winds, and hot Texas weather can quickly turn a small outdoor fire into a major wildfire.

Before conducting any outdoor burning, residents should verify whether burning is currently permitted and whether a burn ban or other restriction is in effect.

When outdoor burning is permitted:

  • Never leave a fire unattended.
  • Have an adequate water source immediately available.
  • Avoid burning during high winds or extremely dry conditions.
  • Keep the burn area clear of surrounding vegetation and combustible materials.
  • Fully extinguish the fire before leaving the area.
  • Follow all applicable state, county, and local regulations.

Always call 911 immediately if a fire gets out of control.

Station Tours & Fire Safety Presentations

The Wills Point Volunteer Fire Department enjoys opportunities to meet members of our community and teach children and adults about fire safety.

Station tours and fire-safety presentations may be available for:

  • Schools and classrooms
  • Daycares
  • Scout groups
  • Churches
  • Civic organizations
  • Community groups
  • Youth organizations

Depending on availability, visitors may have an opportunity to meet firefighters, see fire apparatus and equipment, learn about firefighter protective gear, and discuss fire and emergency safety.

All visits are subject to firefighter and apparatus availability. Because WPFD remains in service during tours and presentations, firefighters may be required to leave at any time to respond to an emergency.

Request WPFD at Your Event

Planning a community event?

Organizations may request the Wills Point Volunteer Fire Department to participate in qualifying community events, including festivals, school functions, public-safety events, Touch-a-Truck events, neighborhood gatherings, and other community programs.

Requests should be submitted as early as possible and should include:

  • Organization or Event Name
  • Event Date and Time
  • Event Location
  • Contact Person
  • Phone Number and Email
  • Type of Participation Requested

Participation is based on department availability and emergency-response requirements. Apparatus and personnel attending an event remain available for emergency response and may be required to leave without notice.

Severe Weather & Emergency Preparedness

Emergencies can happen with little warning. Residents are encouraged to prepare their households before severe weather or other emergencies occur.

Consider keeping an emergency supply kit containing:

  • Drinking water
  • Nonperishable food
  • Flashlights
  • Extra batteries
  • First-aid supplies
  • Necessary medications
  • Phone chargers or portable battery packs
  • Weather radio
  • Copies of important information
  • Supplies for children, elderly family members, and pets

Texas weather can change quickly. Stay aware of current conditions and take watches and warnings seriously.

For an immediate threat to life or property, call 911.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report an emergency?

Call 911 for fires, medical emergencies, vehicle accidents, hazardous conditions, or any situation involving an immediate threat to life or property.

Can I visit the fire station?

Station visits may be arranged depending on department availability. Organized groups are encouraged to contact the department in advance.

Can the Fire Department attend our event?

Yes. Community organizations may submit a request for department participation. Approval depends on personnel and apparatus availability.

Can I donate to the Fire Department?

Yes. Individuals, organizations, and businesses interested in supporting WPFD are encouraged to contact the department for current needs and donation information.

Can firefighters be called away during an event or station tour?

Yes. WPFD remains in service at all times. Personnel and apparatus may be required to immediately leave for an emergency.

How do I know if a burn ban is in effect?

Residents should verify current Van Zandt County outdoor-burning restrictions before burning. Conditions and restrictions can change quickly, particularly during periods of dry or windy weather.

How can I become a volunteer firefighter?

Visit our Membership page to meet our members, and contact the department for membership requirements, application information, and details about serving with the Wills Point Volunteer Fire Department.
