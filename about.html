About Us · Established 1903

Neighbors serving neighbors since 1903.

For more than 120 years, the Wills Point Volunteer Fire Department has proudly served Wills Point and the surrounding areas of Van Zandt County.

What began as a community-based fire department has grown into a modern, all-hazards volunteer fire and rescue organization. In partnership with Van Zandt County Emergency Services District No. 4 (VZC ESD #4), we provide fire suppression, rescue, emergency medical response, wildland firefighting, and other emergency services across approximately 136 square miles.

Our History

A tradition of answering the call.

Established in 1903, Wills Point VFD began a tradition of volunteer service that continues today. Early firefighters worked without the modern protective equipment, communications, breathing apparatus, rescue tools, and fire apparatus available today. They relied on manpower, basic equipment, local water supplies, and citizens willing to help their neighbors.

As Wills Point and the surrounding area grew, the department grew with it. Increasing development, traffic, rural population, and changing emergency-service needs transformed a department focused primarily on fighting fires into today's all-hazards emergency response organization.

VZC ESD #4 · Established 2015

A partnership for the future.

In 2015, voters established Van Zandt County Emergency Services District No. 4, creating a dedicated governmental structure to support fire and emergency services throughout the district.

As call volumes, apparatus costs, equipment needs, training requirements, and community expectations increased, VZC ESD #4 provided a more stable foundation for emergency services. Its Board of Emergency Services Commissioners is appointed by the Van Zandt County Commissioners Court.

Today, VZC ESD #4 and Wills Point VFD work together to provide the stations, apparatus, equipment, and resources needed to protect approximately 136 square miles of Van Zandt County.

Our Main Station

A new chapter on North 5th Street.

For many years, fire operations were centered at the former station at 121 South 4th Street. Generations of Wills Point firefighters responded from its bays, trained there, maintained equipment, and conducted department business within its walls.

As the apparatus fleet and operational needs grew, a larger, more modern facility became necessary. Today, the Main Station is located at 311 North 5th Street in Wills Point.

The move marked an important new chapter for a department serving the community since 1903. The facility supports training, station staffing, emergency response, and day-to-day operations.

Built for today's department

  • Seven apparatus bay doors
  • Five private dorm rooms and showers
  • A dedicated training classroom
  • A modern kitchen
  • Administrative and operational spaces

Our Response Area

Three stations. 136 square miles.

Our response area includes the City of Wills Point and a large surrounding rural area, with Interstate 20, U.S. Highway 80, numerous Farm-to-Market and county roads, and a major railroad corridor passing through the district.

Wills Point VFD and VZC ESD #4 operate from three strategically located stations, positioning emergency resources throughout the district, from south of Interstate 20 to the Lake Tawakoni Dam area.

Station 1

Main Station

311 N 5th Street, Wills Point

Located within the city, the Main Station is the central hub for department operations, training, administration, personnel, equipment, and primary emergency-response resources.

Station 2

Southern District

21716 FM 47, Wills Point

South of Interstate 20 near the district's southern end, Station 2 positions apparatus and equipment closer to the I-20 corridor and rural communities and properties in the southern response area.

Station 3

Northern District

6855 FM 1395, Wills Point

Near the northern end of the district and Lake Tawakoni Dam, Station 3 extends emergency resources into the northern and rural portions of our response area.

Serving Our Community Today

Prepared for more than fires.

Our members respond to structure and commercial fires, grass and wildland fires, motor vehicle collisions and extrications, emergency medical incidents, vehicle fires, hazardous conditions, severe weather, public-assistance calls, and other emergencies.

Training and teamwork

We meet every Thursday at 7 PM at Station 1, 311 N 5th Street. The first Thursday of every month is our business meeting.

Our firefighters regularly train in structural and wildland firefighting, rescue, emergency medical response, hazardous materials, incident command, communications, roadway safety, and other emergency-response disciplines.

We work closely with neighboring fire departments, law enforcement, emergency medical providers, Van Zandt County agencies, the Texas A&M Forest Service, and other local and state partners.

More Than 120 Years of Service

Our legacy. Our community. Our future.

Since 1903, our apparatus, equipment, stations, technology, and responsibilities have changed dramatically. The commitment of the people behind the department has endured.

For generations, Wills Point firefighters have left their families, jobs, meals, holidays, and beds in the middle of the night to answer calls for help. Today, from three stations serving approximately 136 square miles, our volunteers continue the tradition started by those first firefighters more than 120 years ago.

When our community calls, we answer.
