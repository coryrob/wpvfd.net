Neighbors protecting neighbors across Wills Point.

The Wills Point Volunteer Fire Department is an all-volunteer department serving the Wills Point area and VZC ESD #4 with emergency response, preparedness, and a strong local commitment to public safety.

Serving Wills Point

Ready to respond.
Committed to serve.

Our volunteer firefighters protect lives and property in Wills Point and the surrounding community. Through emergency response, ongoing training, and fire prevention, we work to keep our neighbors safe.

01

Emergency response

Responding to structure fires, brush fires, alarms, and other emergencies in our service area.

02

Mutual aid

Working alongside neighboring departments when an emergency calls for additional help.

03

Fire prevention and preparedness

Sharing safety information and connecting with residents to help our community prepare for emergencies.
